History

How the chart is drawn

1 h: every reading (about 3 a second; stored in epever.db when the page runs from epever_monitor.sh), one-minute averages where none. 6 h and 24 h: one-minute averages. Gaps are times the page wasn’t logging.

Daily log

How it’s counted

Ah in/out = net battery current integrated (into / out of the battery), as the controller sees it: loads wired straight to the battery (inverter, heater) are not counted. Solar and load Ah = their power integrated, divided by each minute’s battery voltage. Times are the minute the extreme happened (laptop clock). “Logged” = how much of the day this page was recording; totals cover only that time.

Statistics

How it’s counted

All history in epever.db. Totals cover the time the page was logging (“Logged”); “While off” is what the controller’s lifetime counters gained in the gaps (10 Wh steps). Lowest / highest are single readings with their time; negative current = discharging.

While the laptop was off

From the controller’s lifetime counters, saved on every connection. Resolution 10 Wh.

Capacity and conditioning

How the 50 % cycle works

Green Cell’s remedy for insufficient capacity: charge fully, then discharge to 50 %, 3 to 5 times. Start right after a full charge: solar charging goes off and comes back on at half the capacity setting (Ah out) or when the battery falls to 12.9 V, whichever comes first. If the voltage floor ends it, the battery holds less than its rating; Ah out ÷ 0.8 is a rough real capacity.

Controller settings

read only

Settings are read only. The one thing this page can change is the solar-charging switch at the top (Modbus coil 0x0000). Battery temperature comes from EPEver’s remote sensor when one is fitted to the battery; without it the controller reports its own temperature, and the low-temperature protections act on that.
